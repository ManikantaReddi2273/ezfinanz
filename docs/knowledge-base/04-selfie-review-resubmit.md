# Selfie review, rejection, and resubmit

## After sending the application

When you tap **Send Application**, status becomes pending admin review.

An administrator reviews your selfie and application details.

## Approval

If approved, you wait for disbursement. An admin confirms disbursement to your registered bank account.

You may also receive an email with the review status and any admin message.

## Rejection

If rejected:

- You see the rejection reason in the dashboard / selfie step
- You may receive an email with the admin reason
- You can edit required details, capture a new selfie, confirm it, and **Resubmit Application**

Rejected applications are editable again until you send them for review once more.

## Tips for a clear selfie

- Good lighting
- No cap or glasses that hide your face
- Clear face looking at the camera
