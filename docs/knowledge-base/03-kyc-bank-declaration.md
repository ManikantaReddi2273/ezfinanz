# KYC, bank account, and declaration

## KYC

Provide identity and address details, including:

- Full name and date of birth
- Gender
- ID type and number (for example PAN or Aadhaar)
- Address, city, state, pincode
- Optional ID document upload (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF)

Complete KYC before opening eligibility.

## Bank account

Add the disbursement account:

- Account holder name
- Bank name
- Account number
- IFSC code

This account is used after approval and disbursement confirmation.

## Declaration

Before selfie verification you must accept that:

- Your information is true and accurate
- EZFINANZ may verify identity, credit, bank, and PAN details
- You agree to terms and privacy policy

Accept the declaration to unlock the selfie step.
