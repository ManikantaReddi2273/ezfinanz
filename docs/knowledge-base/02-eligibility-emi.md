# Eligibility and EMI

## Eligibility check

Eligibility uses:

- Income (monthly or annual)
- Requested loan amount
- Credit score (CIBIL)
- Outstanding debts
- Debt-to-income ratio

Possible results:

- **Eligible** — requested amount is within capacity
- **Partially eligible** — a lower amount can still be offered
- **Not eligible** — the profile does not meet product rules

Product loan range is typically ₹25,000 to ₹15,00,000. Monthly income should meet the product minimum (around ₹15,000).

## EMI selection

On EMI Selection you choose tenure (12, 18, 24, or 36 months).

The screen shows:

- Interest rate
- Processing fee and GST
- Monthly EMI
- Total interest and repayment
- Net disbursement

Loan amount is based on eligibility capacity. Continue after confirming EMI terms to add your bank account.
