# EZFINANZ application steps

EZFINANZ is a digital personal loan application with 8 guided steps:

1. Sign Up & Login
2. Verify Email & Phone
3. KYC Details
4. Loan Eligibility
5. EMI Selection
6. Bank Account
7. Declaration
8. Selfie Verification

## Editing before you send

Until you tap **Send Application** on the Selfie Verification step, you can go back and edit completed steps.

If you change an earlier step (for example KYC or EMI), later steps may need to be reviewed again.

## Confirm selfie vs send application

- **Confirm Selfie** saves your photo as a draft.
- **Send Application** submits the full application for admin review.

Do not confuse confirming a selfie with final submission. The application is under review only after Send Application.
