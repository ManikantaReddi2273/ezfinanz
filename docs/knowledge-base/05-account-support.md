# Account verification and support

## Email and phone verification

Both email and phone must be verified before KYC and later loan steps.

Use OTP verification from the Verify Email & Phone step.

## Help & Support

On Help & Support you can:

- Read FAQs
- Chat with the EZFINANZ support assistant (answers from uploaded help docs)
- Send a Contact Support message to the support team by email

If the chatbot cannot answer from the docs, use Contact Support.

## Admin roles (for staff)

- Admins review applications, approve or reject selfies, and confirm disbursement
- Only the super admin can create or delete other admins and manage the chatbot knowledge base
